Demo vs real

Why practice before going live?

  • Demo trading uses virtual funds and helps you learn platform basics.
  • Real-money trading involves emotional pressure, actual losses, and strict controls.
  • Risk-first education helps traders build habits before considering any live account.

Risk warning

Trading forex involves risk.

Past performance and simulated results do not guarantee future returns. Always trade responsibly and only with capital you can afford to lose.
